## 1. Instagram Carousel & Reel Template

### Structure:
- **Line 1-2 (The Scroll Stopper):** Must tease the hook before Instagram hides text behind `...more`.
- **The Core Insight:** 3-4 short, punchy paragraphs separated by clean line breaks.
- **Key Takeaways:** 3 bullet points summarizing the biological facts.
- **The Call To Action:** Direct call to comment, save, share, or visit the profile link/DM.
- **Hashtags:** 25-30 curated tags categorized into:
  - 10 Broad wellness & nutrition tags.
  - 10 Niche product/ingredient tags.
  - 5-10 Regional / Indian health & wellness tags.
