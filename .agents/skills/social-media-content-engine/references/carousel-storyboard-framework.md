# Multi-Slide Carousel Storyboard Framework

This framework structures social media carousel sets (Instagram 4:5 swipeable galleries, LinkedIn PDF carousel decks, YouTube Community carousels) and provides the storyboard foundation for short-form AI video generation (Reels & Shorts).

---

## Storyboard Architecture

```
[Slide 1: Curiosity Hook] 
   └──> [Slide 2 to N: Factual Knowledge & Biological Mechanism]
          └──> [Slide N+1: Creator Recommendation ("From my side...")]
                 └──> [Slide N+2: Truthful Market Comparison ("Why This vs Market")]
                        └──> [Final Slide: Trust & Direct Contact CTA]
```

---

## Slide 1: The Curiosity Hook (The Thumb-Stopper)

The first slide must stop the scroll immediately. It **must** open with one of the approved curiosity angles:

1. **"Do you know this ?"**
   - *Example:* "Do you know this ? Most calcium supplements in India are just ground limestone (chalk) that your body struggles to absorb."
2. **"Don't do this / Don't use this in that way ?"**
   - *Example:* "Don't take your daily multivitamin right after your morning chai! Here is what the tannins are secretly doing."
3. **"How does it work ?"**
   - *Example:* "How does plant protein actually build lean muscle compared to dairy whey? The science will surprise you."
4. **"What is this ?"**
   - *Example:* "What is this dark greenish sediment inside your omega-3 softgel? It reveals whether your fish oil is rancid or pure."

### Visual Direction for Slide 1:
- Arresting macro imagery, high visual contrast, clean negative space.
- Bold, centered question typography with high legibility.
- Visual elements placed strictly within the 4:5 center zone so it translates to 9:16 video without clipping.

---

## Slides 2 to N: Factual Knowledge (The Value Delivery)

The number of educational slides (typically 2 to 4) adapts to the complexity of the subject:
- **Slide 2 (The Underlying Problem / Mechanism):** Explain the biological reality in plain English. Show an anatomical or cellular graphic.
- **Slide 3 (The Science / The 'Why'):** Explain how the body processes or lacks this nutrient. Show natural whole foods vs deficiency indicators.
- **Slide 4 (The Real Requirement / Daily Reality):** Show why modern diets fail to provide the full clinical RDA without smart supplementation.

### Golden Rules for Value Slides:
- **One key insight per slide.** Never crowd a slide with paragraphs. Maximum 20-30 words of prominent overlay text.
- Use visual metaphors (e.g. locks & keys for enzymes, absorption sponge for gut lining).
- Maintain visual continuity: uniform palette, typography style, and brand footer.

---

## Slide N+1: The Promotion Slide (Creator's Recommendation)

Transitions smoothly from objective education to authentic guidance:
- **Header Line:** *"From my side, I would suggest this:"*
- **Hero Element:** Clean studio product packshot of the specific Nutrilite / N-Square product.
- **Key Formulation Highlights:**
  - Certified organic botanicals.
  - Precise active dosage per serving.
  - Zero artificial colors, flavors, or synthetic additives.
- **Tone:** A trusted nutritional advisor recommending what they and their family trust, backed by science.
