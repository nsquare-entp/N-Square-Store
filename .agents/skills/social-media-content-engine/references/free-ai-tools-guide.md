As requested, here is a curated guide of **100% free or generous free-tier AI tools with NO watermarks, commercial rights, and high reliability** for generating social media visuals, bilingual voiceovers, and short-form videos.

## 1. Top Free AI Image Generators (Zero Watermarks & High Quality)

### 🥇 Option 1: Flux.1 Schnell via Free Web Hubs (Top Recommendation)
- **What it is:** State-of-the-art open-source image model (created by Black Forest Labs, ex-Stability AI founders). Produces hyper-realistic botanical details, accurate hands, and cinematic studio lighting.
- **Where to use for free without watermarks:**
  - **Pollinations.ai** (`https://pollinations.ai`): Completely free, instant, unlimited, zero login required, no watermark, commercial license.
  - **Hugging Face Spaces (Flux.1 Schnell)**: Completely free, no watermark, direct high-resolution PNG download.
  - **Together.ai / Fal.ai Free Trial Credits**: Ultra-fast sub-second generation.
- **Best For:** Slide 1 hooks, raw botanical ingredients, macro photography, product textures.

### 🥈 Option 2: Ideogram (Free Daily Credits)
- **What it is:** `https://ideogram.ai`
- **Why it is special:** The **#1 model for rendering crisp, legible typography on images** without spelling gibberish.
- **Watermark:** Zero watermarks on downloaded outputs.
- **Daily Limit:** Gives 10-25 free prompt credits every single day (resets daily).
- **Best For:** Slide 1 typography hooks, split comparison infographics with text headers.

### 🥉 Option 3: Recraft.ai (Free Daily Tier)
- **What it is:** `https://recraft.ai`
- **Why it is special:** Dedicated design tool for graphic designers. Can output raster photography, clean 3D icons, and vector illustrations.
- **Watermark:** No watermark on free downloads.
- **Best For:** Clean biological infographics, cell diagrams, and minimalist comparison charts.

### 🏅 Option 4: Leonardo.ai (Daily Free Credits)
- **What it is:** `https://leonardo.ai`
- **Daily Limit:** 150 free fast tokens replenished every 24 hours (generates ~15-20 images daily).
- **Watermark:** No watermark on full-res downloads.
- **Best For:** High-end commercial studio packshots and realistic product presentations.

## 3. Bilingual AI Voiceover & Video Generation Tools

### ElevenLabs (Free Tier)
- **URL:** `https://elevenlabs.io`
- **Free Allowance:** 10,000 characters per month free.
- **Capabilities:** World-class natural emotional human voice. Supports English and Telugu voice clones/multilingual models.
- **Best For:** Generating the 45-second narration track in English and natural conversational Telugu.

### CapCut (Free Desktop & Mobile)
- **Cost:** 100% free with no watermark (simply delete the 2-second CapCut logo slide at the end of the timeline).
- **Workflow:**
  1. Import the generated 9:16 slide images.
  2. Import the ElevenLabs bilingual audio track.
  3. Set each slide duration to 5-7 seconds with subtle "Ken Burns" pan/zoom transitions.
  4. Click **Auto Captions** -> Generates synchronized animated captions on the screen.
  5. Export 1080x1920 60fps video ready for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels!
