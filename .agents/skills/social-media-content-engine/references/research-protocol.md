# Factual Product Research & Competitive Comparison Protocol

This protocol enforces **absolute truth**, scientific accuracy, and strict compliance with health & wellness marketing regulations (FSSAI/ASCI/Medical standards). **No anticipation, no guesswork, no pseudo-science.**

---

## 1. Internal Truth Mining (Local Catalog & Clinical Data)

Whenever a product is chosen, extract its ground truth directly from the N-Square data assets:
- **`data/official_amway_details.json`**: Official ingredients, formulation specs, dosage, packaging, direct benefits, clinical nutrition rationale.
- **`data/reports_bundle.js`**: Medical standards table, ingredient measurements, RDA percentage coverage, authority standard URLs, and fit assessments.
- **Product Monograph & Labels**: Check organic certified farm origins, phytonutrient concentrations, tablet coating technology, and allergen declarations.

### Critical Verification Checklist:
1. **Active Ingredients & Quantity**: Exact milligrams/grams per serving (e.g. Nutrilite All Plant Protein: 8g protein per 10g scoop, PDCAAS = 1.0, 9 essential amino acids).
2. **Formulation Source**: Natural botanical extract vs synthetic isolate (e.g. Acerola Cherry extract vs synthetic ascorbic acid).
3. **Certifications**: FSSAI approved, NSF International, Non-GMO, certified organic farm traceability.
4. **Bioavailability Mechanism**: How is it absorbed? (e.g. enteric coating, plant-based lipid solubility, timed release).

## 3. Strict Truth & Compliance Boundaries

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Prohibition on Medical Claims:**
> 1. NEVER say "This cures diabetes / cancer / thyroid / hypertension".
> 2. ALWAYS phrase as dietary support: *"Supports healthy cellular function", "Helps bridge the daily nutritional gap", "Nutritionally supports immune resilience"*.
> 3. If an ingredient has a specific peer-reviewed clinical trial, cite the exact scientific mechanism (e.g. *"Clinical trials show EPA & DHA fatty acids support arterial flexibility"*), never make broad unverified claims.
> 4. Ensure RDA (Recommended Daily Allowance) alignment matches ICMR (Indian Council of Medical Research) benchmarks.

---

## 4. USP Extraction Template

For the selected product, summarize the output into 4 core pillars:
1. **The Core Truth**: What is the biological problem it solves?
2. **The Source Superiority**: Why is this specific source cleaner/purer?
3. **The Bioavailability Factor**: Why does the human body absorb this better?
4. **The Market Contrast**: The exact difference between this and cheap mass-market alternatives.
